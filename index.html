Privacy Policy

Sky Stack is published by SanabelApps.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

🛡️ The short version: Sky Stack has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your scores and settings stay on your device, and we receive nothing.

1. What the game stores

Sky Stack saves the following on your device only, using the storage built into the app:

  • Your best height, best streak and total coins
  • Mission progress
  • Sound, vibration and language preferences

2. What we collect

Nothing. Sky Stack has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers. We do not receive your scores, device identifiers, contacts or location.

3. Permissions

The game uses vibration only, and only while the Vibration setting is on. It does not request the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location or phone state.

4. Children

Sky Stack is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal data at all, it is safe for children and aligned with COPPA and GDPR-K by design.

5. Deleting your data

Open Settings → Reset progress to erase scores, coins and missions, or uninstall the app to remove everything. There is nothing stored on our side to delete.

6. Store platform

Google Play may collect install and crash information as part of distributing the app. That data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by SanabelApps.

7. Changes

If a future version adds a feature that needs data — for example online leaderboards — this policy will be updated before that version is released.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach SanabelApps through the developer page on Google Play.
